Wario (Rivals of Aether Workshop) Gallery

My first major Rivals of Aether workshop project, and my first "real" foray into pixel animation that didn't rely solely on rotoscoping. This project took a very long time to finish, but it served as a solid foundation for the kind of work I would produce later.



DISCLAIMER: These creations are not made for profit and are not to be sold. I am not affiliated with Nintendo or any other companies whose properties are present here. These mods are simply created out of love and passion for the source material.

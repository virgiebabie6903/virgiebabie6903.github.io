Sam Sheepdog (Rivals of Aether Workshop) Gallery

Sam Sheepdog was a spur-of-the-moment entry to the Rivals of Aether workshop community's cartoon jam, a month-long jam themed around western animation. Sam is the fastest I have ever produced a fighter, starting development on March 2nd, 2026 and releasing publicly 24 days later.

Sam's placement in the contest results will be posted here when the jam is finished.



DISCLAIMER: These creations are not made for profit and are not to be sold. I am not affiliated with Warner Bros. or any other companies whose properties are present here. These mods are simply created out of love and passion for the source material.

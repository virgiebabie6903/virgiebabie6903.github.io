Yoshi (Rivals of Aether Workshop) Gallery

Yoshi was my second big project for Rivals of Aether workshop that all began from simply noticing that a port of Yoshi from Super Smash Bros. hadn't been added to the game yet.

Despite his similarities to the Smash Bros. version, I took extra care to make sure his animations were cartoony and properly representative of the character people love, taking animation cues from games such as Yoshi's Island and Super Mario Bros. Wonder.

Yoshi is also the first character I made a "properly" animated trailer for, though it is a bit rough compared to what I can make now.



DISCLAIMER: These creations are not made for profit and are not to be sold. I am not affiliated with Nintendo or any other companies whose properties are present here. These mods are simply created out of love and passion for the source material.
